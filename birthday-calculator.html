What is a Birthday Calculator?

A birthday calculator helps you find your next birthday and calculate how much time remains until your birthday. Enter your date of birth to see a live countdown.

Enter your date of birth and click the Start Birthday Countdown button. The calculator automatically finds your next birthday and displays the remaining days, hours, minutes and seconds.
