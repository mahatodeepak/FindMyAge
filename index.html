About Find My Age

Find My Age is a free online age calculator that helps you calculate your exact age in years, months and days.

You can also calculate your age on any date, find your next birthday, see total days lived, use the birthday countdown and check general exam age eligibility.

The calculator works on mobile phones, tablets and computers.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How can I calculate my exact age?

Enter your date of birth and select the date on which you want to calculate your age. The calculator will show your age in years, months and days.

Can I calculate my age on a past or future date?

Yes. Select any valid date in the "Calculate Age As On" field.

How can I find my next birthday?

Enter your date of birth in the Birthday Countdown section. The calculator will show the next birthday and countdown.

Can I use this for exam age eligibility?

Yes, the Exam Age Calculator can provide a general age-limit check. Always verify the official examination notification because cut-off dates and age relaxations can differ.

Is my date of birth stored?

No. This website performs the calculations directly in your browser.

Can I use Find My Age on a mobile phone?

Yes. The website is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets and computers.
