📚 About Find My Age Calculator

Find My Age is a free online age calculator that helps you calculate your exact age in years, months and days. You can also calculate your age on a specific date, find your next birthday and see the total number of days you have lived.

Simply enter your date of birth and choose the date on which you want to calculate your age. The calculator performs the calculation instantly in your browser.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my exact age?

Enter your date of birth in the age calculator and click "Calculate My Age". The calculator will show your age in years, months and days.

Can I calculate my age on a past or future date?

Yes. Enter the date you want to use in the "Age As On" field. You can calculate your age on any valid date after your date of birth.

How can I find my next birthday?

Enter your date of birth and calculate your age. The calculator will show your next birthday and the number of days remaining.

What is an Exam Age Calculator?

The Exam Age Calculator helps you compare your age on a particular cut-off date with a selected maximum age. It can be useful for checking basic age limits for exams.

Is the Exam Age Calculator an official eligibility checker?

No. It only performs a basic age calculation. Official exam eligibility can depend on the latest notification, category-based relaxation and other conditions. Always check the official examination notification.

Is my date of birth stored?

The calculator performs its calculations directly in your browser. Your date of birth is not required to be sent to a server for the basic calculations.

Can I use this calculator on a mobile phone?

Yes. Find My Age is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets and computers.
