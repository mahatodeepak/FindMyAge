What is an Exam Age Calculator?

An exam age calculator helps candidates calculate their age on a particular examination cut-off date.

Many competitive examinations specify a minimum or maximum age. Candidates can use this tool to compare their calculated age with a selected maximum age.

How to Use the Calculator

Enter your date of birth, enter the examination cut-off date and enter the maximum age allowed. Then click "Check Age Eligibility".

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a cut-off date?

A cut-off date is the date specified by an examination authority for determining age or other eligibility requirements.

Can this calculator check SSC eligibility?

It can perform a basic comparison between your age on a selected cut-off date and a maximum age. However, you should always check the official SSC notification for the actual eligibility rules.

Can this calculator include age relaxation?

This basic calculator does not automatically determine category-specific age relaxation. Such rules vary between examinations and notifications.
