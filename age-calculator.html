What is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is an online tool that calculates a person's age from their date of birth. It can show the exact age in years, months and days.

You can also use an age calculator to find your age on a particular date, calculate your next birthday and find the approximate number of days you have lived.

How to Calculate Your Age

Enter your date of birth, select the date on which you want to calculate your age, and press the Calculate My Age button.

The calculator will automatically calculate your years, months and days.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this age calculator?

The calculator uses calendar dates to calculate years, months and days rather than simply dividing the number of days by 365.

Can I calculate my age on another date?

Yes. Enter any valid date after your date of birth in the Age As On field.

Can I find my next birthday?

Yes. The calculator displays your next birthday and the number of days remaining.
